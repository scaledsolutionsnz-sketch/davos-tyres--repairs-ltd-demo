What we do

Tyres & repairs

Your local tyre experts, keeping Southland moving. From everyday vehicles to farm machinery and commercial fleets, we provide quality tyres, expert repairs and trusted service to keep you moving with confidence.

Tyre Sales & Fitting

Tyres for every vehicle and every job, cars, utes, commercial trucks, agricultural machinery, trailers, ATVs and 4WDs. We supply quality tyres and provide expert fitting.

Wheel Balancing

Enjoy a smoother ride and help extend the life of your tyres with precision wheel balancing using our specialist equipment.

Puncture Repairs & Vulcanising

In-house vulcanising and quality repairs you can trust, helping keep you safe on the road with a repair that's built to last.

On-Farm & After-Hours Call-Outs

When you can't get to us, we'll come to you. We provide on-farm repairs and replacements during the day or after hours.

Loan Tyres

Don't let a damaged tyre slow you down. We stock a wide range of loan tyres that can be fitted immediately while your tyre is repaired or a replacement is sourced.

Alloy & Steel Wheels

From tough steel wheels to stylish alloys, we can source a wide range of quality wheels to suit your vehicle and your budget.

Batteries

Quality batteries for a wide range of vehicles and machinery. We can supply, fit and test batteries to keep you moving.

About us

Local people. Honest service.

2019Serving Winton since

Since 2019, Davo's Tyres & Repairs has been proudly keeping Winton and the wider Southland region moving. Locally owned and operated by Adam and Rochelle Davis, we're backed by an experienced team that understands the demands of Southland roads, farms and businesses.

When you're off the road, every minute counts. That's why we're committed to getting you back up and running as quickly as possible, with quality workmanship, fair pricing and honest, reliable service.

Whether you need a puncture repaired, a new set of tyres fitted and balanced, batteries, or an on-farm call-out, we have the equipment, experience and expertise to get the job done right, from cars and utes to trucks, tractors and farm machinery.
